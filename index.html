    Leveling route, 1 to 60

    The level ranges and zones come from the AccountShark route guide, which is generic and has no Horde-specific route. I adapted the sequence for a Tauren Hunter. The Alliance zones that appear in the source (Dun Morogh, Westfall, Darkshore) were removed. Treat the route as a starting point and confirm zones on the in-game map. I did not find zone quest names in my search, so the checklist uses only what the sources mention.

    Weapons and gear

    I only list items cited by sources. I did not find a confirmed weapon-by-level table for Tauren Hunter, so the level and source of each item stay Not confirmed where data is missing. The rule from the sources: slower bows and guns are usually better for Hunters, because damage per shot is calculated from the weapon.

    Pet system

    The pet system in Forever is not the same as Classic. Pets take stats from the Hunter's gear and no longer receive player stat buffs. Each family has a unique ability. There are 18 families at launch, including the new Fox family.

      Taming rules

        Families and ranking

        Rankings differ between sites. Exitlag puts Bear as the best pet for Beast Mastery leveling. Axeetech, which updated its ranking on November 10, puts Carrion Bird as the best for leveling. Both agree Wolf is strong for dungeons. Wowhead does not publish a ranking, only family and ability lists. WoFW recommends Bear for leveling, Wolf for groups, and Crocolisk, Bat, or Bird of Prey for PvP.

        Talents and specialization

        Recommendation: Beast Mastery for leveling. Method says it is the preferred option, especially for solo leveling. Method also notes that the level 60 talent section was not yet complete on their page.

        Leveling build: 31 Beast Mastery, 20 Marksmanship, 0 Survival Confirmed

        51 points total between levels 10 and 60. Bestial Wrath at level 40 completes the Beast Mastery tree. The final 20 points go into Marksmanship.

          Survival: is it worth it?

          Sources differ. Icy Veins calls Survival the worst option for leveling, while Mobalytics says Survival is at the top of the damage charts. For leveling, Beast Mastery is still the safest choice according to the leveling sources. Marksmanship with Lone Wolf was reported as buggy by players.

          Dungeons

          Data from the Warcraft Tavern table. Repeating dungeons after completion gives much less XP than open world. In total there are 9 new dungeons in Forever and 20 classic ones. The maximum group size and loot were not on the page, so they stay Not confirmed. Blizzard also fixed an issue so each enemy killed in a dungeon gives 50 percent more XP (hotfix of November 9).
